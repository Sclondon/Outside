# The hyena's voice

Every sound here is a real recording of a hyena, cut from the recordings listed below by
`tools/cut_hyena_audio.py` (which has the exact stretch of each recording that each file is),
faded in and out, brought to mono, 32 kHz, 16 bits, and brought to a common loudness for its kind.
They are imported uncompressed (`compress/mode=0` in each `.import`), as the hounds' are, so that the game
can read how loud each is through its length to open the hyena's mouth.

**They are recordings of the spotted hyena (*Crocuta crocuta*), not of the striped hyena (*Hyaena hyaena*) that
the game's animal is.** No recording of a striped hyena under a licence that allows this was found. The striped
hyena is much the quieter of the two: it has a chattering laugh and a howl, and does not whoop
(https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Striped_hyena). So the giggles here stand in for its laugh fairly, and the whoops
are borrowed from its cousin for the sake of how they sound across a desert at dusk. When free recordings of a
striped hyena are found, put them here under the same names.

## Attribution required

Both licences require credit, and that changes are said. All of these files are adapted: cut, resampled to mono
at 32 kHz, faded and made louder.

`giggle_1.wav` to `giggle_8.wav` are adapted from Additional files 1 to 8 of:
Mathevon N, Koralek A, Weldele M, Glickman SE, Theunissen FE (2010). "What the hyena's laugh tells: Sex, age,
dominance and individual signature in the giggling call of *Crocuta crocuta*". BMC Ecology 10:9.
https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6785-10-9. Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 2.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/).

`whoop_1.wav` to `whoop_3.wav` are adapted from "Spotted Hyaena (Crocuta crocuta)", British Library wildlife
recording W1CDR0000381 BD12, recorded by David Watts, provided by the British Library through Wikimedia Commons.
Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

To ship without any attribution duty, delete the `.wav` files here: the hyenas are then silent.
