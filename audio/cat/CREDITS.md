# The cat's voice

Every sound here is a real recording of a domestic cat, cut from the recordings listed below by
`tools/cut_cat_audio.py` (which has the exact stretch of each recording that each file is),
faded in and out, brought to mono, 32 kHz, 16 bits, and brought to a common loudness for its kind.
They are imported uncompressed (`compress/mode=0` in each `.import`), as the hounds' are, so that the game
can read how loud each is through its length.
