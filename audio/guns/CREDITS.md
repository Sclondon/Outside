# The guns' sounds

Every sound here is a real recording, cut from the recordings listed below by
`tools/cut_gun_audio.py` (which has the exact stretch of each recording that each file is),
brought to mono, 44.1 kHz, 16 bits, faded in and out, and brought to a common level for its kind.
The shots were also passed through a soft limiter (their first millisecond rounded off under 0.89).
All of it is CC0 / public domain: nothing here carries an attribution duty.
